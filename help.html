BRS Community Chat

Your private space to reach fellow participants — before, during, and after the symposium. Here's everything you need in five minutes.

Open the chat tlab-utokyo.github.io/brs-chat

Step by step

Getting in

  1. Enter the invite password the organizers sent you, and tick the box to accept the community terms. You only do this once per browser.
  2. Sign in — either Sign in with Google (fastest), or request a one-time email link. The link comes from noreply@biohybrid-robotics.com, works for 60 minutes, and must be opened in the same browser.
  3. Add your name and affiliation so people recognize you. Please use your real name.

You'll land in #general — the room everyone shares. Say hi 👋

Find your way around

Channels, teams & direct messages

# Channels

Open topic rooms anyone can join. #general has everyone; use Browse to find more, or + to start one.

🔒 Teams

Private, invite-only rooms — visible only to the people added to them.

Direct messages

Pick one person for a 1-to-1, several for a group, or just yourself for private notes. Only members can read a DM.

The essentials

Saying something

  • ›
    Send with Ctrl + Enter (⌘ + Enter on Mac). A plain Enter just starts a new line.
  • ›
    Mention someone with @name to notify them; @channel notifies the whole room (use sparingly).
  • ›
    Attach up to 10 photos or files — click the clip or just drag & drop. PDFs and Office docs preview in the browser.
  • ›
    React, reply in threads, pin, and save messages to read later — the icons appear when you hover a message.
  • ›
    Jump anywhere with Ctrl + K, search with /, or type /help for the full list.

Get notified when you're away

Get pinged by Slack, Teams, Discord, or email

Forward your BRS DMs and @mentions to an app you already keep open — or just get a plain email. Set it up once; it all keeps working even when the chat tab is closed.

Slack calls this an Incoming Webhook — a private address that lets BRS Chat drop messages into a channel of yours.

  1. In Slack, make a private channel just for you (e.g. #brs-alerts) so only you see the forwarded messages.
  2. Open my.slack.com/services/new/incoming-webhook, choose that channel, and click Add Incoming WebHooks integration.
  3. Copy the Webhook URL (it starts with https://hooks.slack.com/services/…).
  4. In BRS Chat, click your name → Notification settings, paste it in the Slack box, hit Save, then Send a test.

Blocked by your workspace? Ask an admin to add the Incoming WebHooks app — the resulting URL works the same.

Teams does this with a Workflow — a personal address that lets BRS Chat post into a channel of yours. Your work (e.g. university) Teams is fine; no account linking involved.

  1. Pick or create a Teams channel for yourself.
  2. Click the channel's ••• menu → Workflows → pick "Post to a channel when a webhook request is received" (on some tenants it's called "Send webhook alerts to a channel") → Next → Add workflow.
  3. Copy the URL it shows (it contains logic.azure.com or powerplatform.com).
  4. In BRS Chat → your name → Notification settings, paste it in the Teams box, Save, then Send a test.

Older Teams? If your channel menu still offers Connectors → Incoming Webhook, that URL works too.

Discord has webhooks built into every channel's settings.

  1. Make a private channel only you can see.
  2. Open Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook, then Copy Webhook URL.
  3. In BRS Chat → your name → Notification settings, paste it in the Discord box, Save, then Send a test.

The simplest option — no URL, no other app. Just switch it on in the chat.

  1. In BRS Chat, click your name → Notification settings.
  2. Under Email me when I'm away, tick @mentions, @channel, and/or direct messages.
  3. Hit Save. Emails arrive from noreply@biohybrid-robotics.com, and only when you're not currently in the chat — so you're never pinged twice.

First email in your spam / junk folder? Mark it "not spam" so the rest land in your inbox.

Choose what reaches you — direct messages, personal @mentions, @channel broadcasts, or everything — right where you set each one up.

Just want pop-ups in your browser? Notification settings also has plain browser notifications for DMs, mentions, and @channel — no setup beyond clicking Allow. On iPhone, open the site in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen first, then open it from that icon.

Good to know

A few house rules

  • •
    Posts and attachments are visible to everyone in that channel or DM and are kept long-term. Sharing unpublished work is your own call — consider who's in the room. Posted something by mistake? Delete it.
  • •
    Be respectful. Harassment or discriminatory posts aren't allowed and can lead to removal.
  • •
    The service may be retired after the symposium; the organizers aren't liable for posted content or data loss.

If something's off

Trouble getting in?

  • •
    Email link didn't arrive? Check spam. It expires in 60 minutes and must be opened in the same browser — moving phone → PC means you'll be asked to retype your email.
  • •
    Wrong password, or not sure you're on the list? Contact the organizers.
  • •
    Screen looks stuck? A hard refresh (Ctrl + Shift + R) almost always fixes it.
Questions? Just ask in #general — someone's usually ● online and happy to help.
